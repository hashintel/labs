`BeginService` needs a customer in `Waiting` and a free member of staff in `FreeStaff`. Under clocks, what does an empty input place do to its clock?

Here the transition decides: its clock runs only while both places hold a token, and it fires only then.

```python
fires_BeginService = (clk_BeginService == 0) & (Waiting != 0) & (FreeStaff != 0)
```

Zeroth's own `birth_death.py` lets the places decide: the clock always runs, and at expiry each input place gives a token if it has one. With one input the two rules agree, since an exponential delay has no memory. With two they do not: `BeginService` could take a customer while `FreeStaff` is empty.
