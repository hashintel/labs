`BeginService` needs a customer in `Waiting` and a free member of staff in `FreeStaff`. Under coins that is two tests in one guard. Under clocks the compiler must also decide what an empty input does to the clock.

Under **Rates** on coin, the default, `Transition_BeginService` tests both places beside its draw:

```python
return (Waiting >= 1.0) & (FreeStaff >= 1.0) & (X(u_BeginService) >= 0.6703200460356393)
```

Under **Rates** on clock, the transition decides. `fires_BeginService` keeps both tests, and the clock runs down only while both places hold a token:

```python
fires_BeginService = (clk_BeginService == 0) & (Waiting != 0) & (FreeStaff != 0)
```

Zeroth's own `birth_death.py` lets the places decide instead. The clock always runs, and at expiry each input place gives a token if it has one. With one input place the two rules agree. With two they do not: `BeginService` could take a customer from `Waiting` while `FreeStaff` is empty.

### Options

- **Rates** on clock gives the SPN modules above.
- **Marking** on Int keeps the places Int, and the guard reads `X(hit_BeginService)`, a Bool from `Draw_BeginService`, in place of the draw.
