Under clocks a conflict is a race: `TakeLeft` and `TakeRight` both take from `Pool`, and the clock that reaches 0 first takes the token.

<Figure caption="TakeLeft and TakeRight race for Pool. The first clock to reach 0 fires, takes a token and re-arms; the other keeps its time. With Pool empty, both wait.">
  <ClockRace />
</Figure>

With **Conflicts** on nondet, a taker also needs its pick at expiry. A false pick stops time until it turns true.

```python
fires_TakeLeft = (clk_TakeLeft == 0) & (Pool != 0) & pick_TakeLeft
```

`Place_Pool` applies one firing at a time, each case needing the other events off. So two takers that expire together both produce, while `Pool` loses no token.

### Options

- **Conflicts** on sweep drops the picks: the first clock to expire fires.
