A conflict involves only the transitions that share an input place. `TakeLeft` and `TakeRight` both take from `Pool`, while `Return` takes from `Left`, which no other transition takes from.

So **Conflicts** on nondet gives a pick to `TakeLeft` and `TakeRight` and none to `Return`. `Transition_TakeLeft` adds `X(pick_TakeLeft)` to its test, and `Transition_Return` keeps its plain guard, `Left >= 1`.

A property proved under nondet holds for every way the picks resolve the conflict, while `Return` fires whenever `Left` holds a token. Under the default, **Conflicts** on sweep, record order decides: `TakeRight` tests `avail_Pool`, the tokens `TakeLeft` left.

### Options

- **Conflicts** on nondet adds `pick_TakeLeft` and `pick_TakeRight`.
- **Shape** on monolithic gives one `Fork` class whose `next` takes from `Pool` in record order.
