Tokens can carry attributes that kernels write. A `Drone` has a real `battery` and a `state`, `idle` or `flying`. `Launch` fires at a rate that reads the battery and writes a flying drone into `Airborne`. `Drain` lowers the battery of every airborne drone, and `Land` brings a drone back with a full battery.

### Refused here

The rate, the guard, the two kernels and the equation are code strings. So the compiler refuses the net with `code-not-parsed`: five diagnostics for three items.

With a parser, the monolithic step lays each coloured place out as a row of positions. Each position has a present flag and one variable per attribute: `Hangar_0_present`, `Hangar_0_battery` and `Hangar_0_state`. The string `state` is held as a Real code, 0 for `idle` and 1 for `flying`. A kernel writes locals named `out_Launch_Airborne_0_*`, which land after the surviving tokens close up.

**Shape** on modular refuses the net with `modular-coloured-not-lowered` alone, before it reads any code.
