`Infection` takes one `Susceptible` and one `Infected` and gives back two `Infected`: an output arc of weight 2. Coins compile any weight. Clocks refuse it, because an SPN event moves one token per arc.

In the monolithic shape, `Infection` takes its inputs at once, and its two tokens land at the end of the step:

```python
Infected = ite(fire_Infection, Infected - 1.0, Infected)
Infected = ite(fire_Infection, Infected + 2.0, Infected)
```

In the modular shape, `Place_Infected` nets the two into one line:

```python
Infected = ite(X(fire_Infection), Infected + 1.0, Infected)
```

The rates are constants, $\beta = 3$ and $\gamma = 1$. So `Infection` fires at rate 3 whatever the marking, where mass action would use $\beta \cdot S \cdot I$.

### Options

- **Rates** on clock refuses the net with `clocks-arc-weight`.
- **Shape** on monolithic gives the take and the landing above.
