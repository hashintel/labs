A coloured place becomes a row of positions. `Pool` holds two `Ball` tokens with a real `x`, and the rate of `Take` reads the first token: $\lambda = 0.6 \cdot x / 100$.

### Refused here

The rate is code, so the compiler refuses the net with `code-not-parsed`.

With a parser, the monolithic step gives each position of `Pool` a present flag and an `x`: `Pool_0_present` and `Pool_0_x`. `Take` tries the tokens in order and fires on the first whose rate reaches an exponential draw, `e_Take`. At the end of the step the surviving tokens close up in position order.

**Shape** on modular refuses the net with `modular-coloured-not-lowered`, before it reads any code.
