How should SPN express an arc of weight 2: `(n + 1) + 1`, an `Inc` of `Inc`, or a count on `Inc` and `Dec`?

### Today

Under coins an arc of weight w tests `P >= w` and moves w tokens at once:

```python
Parts = ite(X(fire_Join), Parts - 2, Parts)  # Join takes 2
```

Under clocks an arc of weight above 1 is refused with `clocks-arc-weight`: SPN tests a count against zero and moves one token per arc, `P != 0` and `Pool + 1`.

### Options on the table

- Nest the increments, `(n + 1) + 1`, an `Inc` of `Inc`. `check_nat_ops` suggests it types; it is untested.
- A count on `Inc` and `Dec`, so one event moves w tokens.
- Refuse, as today.
