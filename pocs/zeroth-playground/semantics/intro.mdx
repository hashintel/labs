## Semantics

This view holds what a net means once it is compiled to modules, and what is still open between HASH and Zeroth: what one step is, who settles a choice the net leaves open, what a place can express under clocks, how a rate becomes a firing. Each question quotes the Python the compiler writes today and the answers on the table. Questions about tooling, such as executors and seeds, stay in `compiler/mapping.md`.

A question names who can settle it, HASH, Zeroth or both, and its status: open, proposed, or settled with a decision and its date. Under it, "Shows in" compiles an example at the options that bring the behaviour out and quotes the lines of the net item that carry it; "Open in Playground" opens that example with those options.
