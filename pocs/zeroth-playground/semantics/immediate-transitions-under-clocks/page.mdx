How does a transition without a rate fire under clocks? Under coins it fires every step it is enabled. Under clocks every transition needs a clock, and a clock needs a rate.

### Today

Under coins a plain transition is a predicate on the marking, tested once per step:

```python
fire_Go = A >= 1  # Go: A -> B
```

Under clocks a transition without a rate is refused with `clocks-plain-transition`, so a mixed net compiles under coins only.

### Options on the table

- A zero-delay construct in SPN, with priorities among the immediate transitions that are enabled at one instant.
- A very high rate as an approximation, which leaves a small chance that a timed transition fires first.
- Refuse, as today.
